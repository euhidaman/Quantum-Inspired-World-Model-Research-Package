## Recommendation

[HYPOTHESIS] Give PSC the first engineering opportunity only after Stage 0 validates its dependency representation, canonical truncation diagnostic and matched-budget utility. Retain QLC as a small formal comparator. Defer TEC until persistent branch information is given an observable operational meaning and beats equally persistent classical particles. This is a risk-based research allocation, not a measured ranking.

[INFERENCE] PSC supplies the clearest robotics question: can an explicitly limited correlation structure help filtering and planning when obstacle interactions are sparse and variable? It also supplies a measurable failure mode when chain order or limited bond capacity mismatches scene dependencies. This argument warrants cheap falsification, not a full simulator implementation.

## QLC: established family, restricted expressiveness statement

[ESTABLISHED] QLC's density/Kraus sequence backbone is HQMM prior art. Existing work learns these models on classical computers and expresses them as OOMs. See [Monras et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1002.2337), [Srinivasan et al. 2018](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v84/srinivasan18a.html) and [Adhikary et al. 2020](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v108/adhikary20a.html). More latent coordinates than a d-way categorical distribution do not establish fair superiority.

[ESTABLISHED] A key current correction is that the 2020/2021 open question about finite HQMM versus general finite linear models has since been resolved. [Fanizza, Lumbreras and Winter 2024](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00220-023-04913-4) construct finite general linear processes with no finite-dimensional HQMM realization (§2.4). Finite HMM, HQMM and quasi-realization process classes are strictly nested in their stationary finite-alphabet exact setting. Finite linear state does not mean finite classical hidden-state simplex.

[INFERENCE] Neither this hierarchy nor quantum-HMM memory-compression studies compare classical RAM, training complexity or navigation regret. A d-dimensional quantum-memory accounting cannot be transferred to a simulator storing d² matrix entries. The [2026 compression study](https://journals.aps.org/pra/abstract/10.1103/plt9-cm1x) reports toy and speech examples against simple state merging, at abstract-level verification here.

[ESTABLISHED] [PSRs](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2001/file/1e4d36177d71bbb3558e43af9577d70e-Paper.pdf) and [OOMs](https://www.ai.rug.nl/minds/uploads/oom_neco00.pdf) provide classical predictive-state prior art. The latter author manuscript includes a 2017 correction to Theorem 6(a2); claims relying on the older learning theorem need that correction. The PSR proceedings HTML omits Singh, who is present in the official PDF; the PDF supplies the database author list.

[INFERENCE] QLC's defensible robotics contribution would require an identified intervention/readout span where its restrictions aid estimation under matched resources. The same-diagonal toy task only verifies representational operationality: a one-scalar classical control reproduces it. Gauge-transforming all states/operators leaves predictions unchanged, so arbitrary off-diagonal visualizations are not physical interpretability.

## PSC: established representation, testable robotics specialization

[ESTABLISHED] Positive locally purified representations and channel/truncation methods already exist [Werner et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1412.5746), as do probabilistic tensor models comparing nonnegative, Born and purified factorizations [Glasser et al.](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2019/file/b86e8d03fe992d1b0e19656875ee557c-Paper.pdf). PSC must include nonnegative tensor-train and graph-factor baselines; comparing only independent slots confounds structured dependence with purification.

[ESTABLISHED] Uniform non-terminating locally purified sequence models coincide with HQMMs under [Adhikary et al. 2021, Theorem 5.1](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v130/adhikary21a.html). A finite object-slot chain is a different use of the factorization; specify whether bonds index objects, time or both before invoking equivalence. Do not present PSC and QLC as universally unrelated representations.

[INFERENCE] The chain correlation limit is valid when proved at the purification level: one cut gives Schmidt rank ≤χ, and local partial trace plus strong subadditivity gives I(A:B)≤2logχ. quantum_math.md supplies the proof and multi-edge generalization. This says nothing by itself about how much correlation real pedestrian scenes need. A shared latent context or unrestricted joint decoder can bypass the intended capacity limitation.

[ESTABLISHED] Tensor BP and gauge fixing are established methods. [Alkabetz and Arad](https://journals.aps.org/prresearch/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevResearch.3.023073) distinguish exact tree contraction from uncontrolled loopy approximation. [Tindall and Fishman](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.17837) develop BP gauges. Small residuals or convergence on a loop do not establish accurate marginals.

[HYPOTHESIS] A useful PSC contribution could be a reproducible, correlation-controlled dynamic-obstacle benchmark plus an adaptive allocation rule whose effect on joint calibration and risk survives generic uncertainty alarms and equal-compute capacity increases. Slot ordering, reorder cost, long-range interactions, occlusion and variable cardinality are potential failures to expose. The alarm must use canonical purification residuals, not gauge-dependent raw tensor norms.

## TEC: extra branch memory must be explicit

[ESTABLISHED] Pure-state ensemble decompositions of one density matrix are nonunique; [HJW's author-institution record](https://research.gold.ac.uk/id/eprint/31593/) verifies the classification paper and abstract. Full original text was not retrieved. The operational equivalence statement used here is proved directly in quantum_math.md and does not rely on an uninspected theorem statement.

[INFERENCE] If two ensembles share ρ, common linear instruments and readouts give the same entire observation-history distribution, including history-adaptive action policies. Branch-dependent behavior must therefore add accessible labels, histories, transition rules or nonlinear branchwise maps. These can be valid design choices, but they require the same information and retention budget in classical particle/mixture controls.

[ESTABLISHED] [Stochastic wave-function unravellings](https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.68.580) and [projective evolution geometry](https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.65.1697) are existing mathematics. Publisher abstracts were inspected; the review does not claim detailed full-text audit of those originals.

[INFERENCE] Fubini–Study phase invariance is narrower than ensemble-decomposition invariance. Two equivalent mixtures can yield different branch-diversity scores. Test random phase gauges and branch-label permutations separately from the same-ρ equivalence test. If only persistent classical identity matters, recommend a particle/history model and treat projective geometry as an optional ablation.

## Strongest novelty boundary

The evidence rules out novelty claims for density-state sequence prediction, Kraus-constrained learning, purification-based positivity, tensor BP, BP gauging, ensemble nonuniqueness, unravellings or Fubini–Study geometry by themselves. It does not establish that nobody has combined selected tools for navigation. A supported novelty claim must be narrower: an implemented robotics-specific representation/inference/intervention whose measurable value survives the relevant classical structured baseline under explicit resource constraints. No retrieved source proves that this proposed system meets that bar.
