## Publication decision

The December RSS 2027 first-stage process is a possible calendar checkpoint, not a recommendation to submit an unvalidated idea. Evaluate by mid-November whether Stage 0 has produced a concrete, correct mechanism, a defensible novelty boundary and a credible extension plan. Otherwise continue toward a later robotics cycle or RA-L after evidence exists. A near deadline must not replace the gates. Future CoRL/ICRA/IROS/ML-cycle rules require a fresh official check; none is assumed here.

One evidence base should yield one principal archival submission. A later journal extension needs materially new theory/algorithm/evaluation, citation and disclosure of the earlier paper, and explicit compatibility with both venues. The TEC/vision/ML blueprints are alternative tracks unless they develop genuinely distinct contributions. No external submission, registration or contact has been made.
