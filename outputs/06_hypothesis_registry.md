Proposed engineering thresholds, not empirical facts: NLL improvement 0.01 nat per scored outcome for a pilot screening signal; impossible-mode error <=0.01 in an exact two-mode task; downstream absolute collision-risk reduction of at least 0.01 with success loss no worse than 0.02. These thresholds are decision preferences, require mentor/domain ratification before confirmatory data, and are accompanied by sensitivity analyses. No observed effect will be used to retroactively choose them.

For mechanistic identities, use exact analytical targets and numerical tolerances, not statistical significance. For learned models, uncertainty is across independent training runs and scenario blocks, not time steps. Stage 0 chooses one candidate; final primary comparison is that frozen candidate against the strongest eligible classical model selected on validation data under the same budget. Secondary comparisons use Holm correction within predeclared families.

Selection rule: continue only if a representational mechanism is correct, a useful gap remains against joint classical baselines, and latency/storage are credible. Failure to reject the null is inconclusive when precision is poor; a tight interval excluding the minimally useful benefit supports stopping. A negative-result paper requires a robust boundary or diagnosis, not merely a failed optimizer run.
