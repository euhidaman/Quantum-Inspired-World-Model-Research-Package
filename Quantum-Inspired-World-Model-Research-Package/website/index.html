What shape is the model’s memory?

A latent space is the model’s internal map of possible situations. These views show how that map may be structured—not what the physical room looks like.

Important: every view is a 3D teaching projection of a much higher-dimensional space, not its complete literal shape.

WHAT YOU ARE SEEING

A compressed state on a learned manifold

The surface groups similar room situations. The path shows how an action moves the state, while uncertainty remains local around that path.

Capacity: a vector has d real coordinates. Its useful geometry is learned from data.

“Bigger” means that more independent values or relationships can be represented—not that the picture should become a physically larger object.

Research boundary. This demonstration compares structures, not benchmark results. A larger state is useful only if it improves prediction or planning at a matched compute budget.

No qubits or quantum hardware are used. The quantum proposal uses density-matrix mathematics on ordinary computers.
